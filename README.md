# Gaytri Market

**Gaytri Market** is a hyperlocal, multi-category e-commerce platform designed specifically for villages and small towns. It connects local shops and businesses with nearby customers, allowing people to order a wide range of legally permitted products from stores within their local area.

Unlike traditional e-commerce platforms that often rely on centralized warehouses and long-distance delivery, Gaytri Market focuses on **local shops, local products, and local delivery**. Shopkeepers can create their own digital store, list products, manage prices and inventory, and receive online orders directly from customers in their service area.

Customers can discover nearby products based on their location, compare prices and estimated delivery times, and place orders through the platform. Local delivery partners can then collect the products from the shop and deliver them to the customer using bikes.

Gaytri Market uses a **dynamic delivery-zone system** based on road distance, estimated travel time, shop availability, and delivery-partner availability. Nearby orders can be targeted for fast delivery, while the platform can support longer local deliveries when they can realistically be completed within the maximum **3-hour delivery window**.

The platform will support multiple product categories, including grocery, clothing, electronics, mobile accessories, stationery, home and kitchen products, hardware, beauty products, agricultural supplies, and other legally permitted products.

Gaytri Market will also support **new and second-hand products**. Used products will have a clearly visible **“Second-Hand”** badge so customers can easily distinguish them from new products.

Beyond online shopping, Gaytri Market aims to strengthen the **local economy** by helping existing shopkeepers reach more customers and creating earning opportunities for local people through delivery services.

### Core Idea

**Local Shops + Local Products + Local Customers + Local Delivery = Gaytri Market**

### Vision

To digitally connect local markets with their communities and make shopping faster, more convenient, and more accessible for people living in villages and small towns.
